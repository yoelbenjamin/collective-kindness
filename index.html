We exist so no one has to face the hardest moments alone.

We help Jewish families with debt. We help Jewish families individuals Shluchim couples professionals with debt gambling addiction divorce housing insecurity career transitions emotional challenges financial pressure legal advocacy .

Founded 2023 · 501(c)(3) non-profit organization

In the Jewish community, the deepest financial struggles are the ones nobody talks about. The debt that grows in private. The gambling nobody knows about. The community leader who can’t admit they’re underwater. What’s missing is a place for the ones who are in the messy middle.

Four pathways,
one organization.

Each program is uniquely designed for different needs and experiences.

Kosher Debt Help
Honest debt guidance from people who genuinely want to help. No commissions, no kickbacks, no shortcuts. $50 program (subsidized from $350) · OU partnered
Visit →
Shluchim Thrive
Financial stability and tailored support for Chabad emissaries, built by people with Shlichus experience. Continental US · Confidential · Advisory board of rabbanim
Visit →
The Right Bet
Gambling addiction recovery for Jewish individuals and families. Confidential, dignified, never alone. Anonymous · 24/7 crisis line · White-glove referrals
Visit →
Local Chapters
In-person community in seven cities: Atlanta, Baltimore, Chicago, Denver, Kingston PA, Long Island, South Florida. Volunteer-led · Locally rooted · Nationally supported

Is Collective Kindness right for me?

If you're a Jewish family or individual carrying a hard chapter — debt, divorce, a job change, a quieter struggle no one knows about — there's a good chance we can walk it with you. Here's some of what that looks like:

  • Mental health counselling
  • Financial coaching
  • Employment consulting
  • Therapy subsidies
  • Legal advocacy
  • A trusted referral network

The journey

Six steps, taken together.

We don't drop a check and disappear. Each family is met by a case manager who walks the road from intake to stability — for as long as it takes.

01 — Apply

Reach out

Submit an application. We review for fit, with respect and dignity.

02 — Intake

Get heard

A confidential conversation about where you are and where you want to be.

03 — Plan

Build a path

Together we design a roadmap rooted in your circumstances, not a template.

04 — Connect

Find your people

We pair you with coaches, counselors, and partner organizations.

05 — Support

Keep walking

Ongoing accompaniment — check-ins, accountability, course corrections.

06 — Thrive

Stand on your own

You graduate when stability is yours to keep.

Your gift becomes someone else's foundation.

Every dollar funds case management, coaching, and the long quiet work of helping a family stand on its own. Designate to a specific program, or trust us to put it where it's needed most.
